# Shields
Data for shield generators are stored in `src/StarXelem/datafiles/libs/foundry/records/entities/scitem/ships/shieldgenerator/`.

## Properties
- **Size**: Located in `SAttachableComponentParams` -> `AttachDef` -> `Size`.
- **Grade**: Located in `SAttachableComponentParams` -> `AttachDef` -> `Grade`.
- **Max Health**: Located in `SCItemShieldGeneratorParams` -> `MaxShieldHealth`.
- **Max Regen**: Located in `SCItemShieldGeneratorParams` -> `MaxShieldRegen`.
- **Resistance**: Located in `SCItemShieldGeneratorParams` -> `ShieldResistance`.
- **Absorption**: Located in `SCItemShieldGeneratorParams` -> `SShieldAbsorption`.
